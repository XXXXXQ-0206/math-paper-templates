\subsection{Deferred computation}

Collect here the computations that are mechanical but long: changes of
variables, sign checks, bookkeeping of constants. Keep each one
self-contained, with its hypotheses restated, so a reader can check it
without rereading the body.

\begin{lemma}
\label{lem:appendix}
With the notation of \cref{sec:main-result}, the constant in
\cref{eq:main} is sharp.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Exhibit a sequence of admissible functions for which the ratio of the
two sides tends to $1$. Details are routine.
\end{proof}
