\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

Present the alternative argument. Say clearly which hypotheses are
weakened relative to the principal manuscript, and which are not.

\begin{theorem}[Alternative proof]
\label{thm:alt}
The conclusion of \cite[Theorem 1.1]{principal} holds whenever
$\mu(X) \le 1$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Repeat the argument of the principal manuscript with the constant
carried explicitly; it is the factor $\mu(X)$ that is relaxed here.
\end{proof}
